Betterbird is a fine-tuned version of Mozilla Thunderbird, Thunderbird on
steroids, if you will.
Betterbird is better than Thunderbird in three ways: It contains new
features exclusive to Betterbird, it contains bug fixes exclusive to
Betterbird and it contains fixes that Thunderbird may ship at a later stage.
Betterbird is a soft fork of Mozilla Thunderbird, closely following the
Thunderbird releases, therefore avoiding the mistakes of other forks which
quickly lost track of upstream Thunderbird.
